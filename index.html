Secrets of Balatonfüred

A CodeChecker Teambuilding

Welcome Explorers!

Get ready to uncover the hidden gems of Balatonfüred in this CodeChecker Team Building adventure. Work together to navigate this part of the city, solve clues, and complete challenges.

📜 The Rules of the Quest:

  • Exact Answers: Type carefully! You have to give the exact answers to each of the questions! Text boxes will instantly light up green when type in the correct answer.
  • Photo Evidence: Some tasks require a photo. Snap them with your phone's camera and keep them ready for the finish line review.
  • Watch the Clock: Your time is tracked from the moment you hit 'Start Quest'. The fastest team with the most correct answers wins!
  • Case and Accents: Casing matters! Always start your answers upper-case and don't use accents, only the english alphabet!

When your entire team is ready at the starting location, press the button below to begin your timer and reveal your first tasks. When finished, come back here. Good luck!
